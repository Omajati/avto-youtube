First, the listen-once test: imagine you hear this script once, with your eyes closed. In retell_ru retell in 1-2 plain Russian sentences what happens, who the hero is, why and how it ends. If you cannot do it simply, or have to guess, clarity and spoken are at most 5 and you must rewrite the script so the story is clear.

Score each criterion from 1 to 10 (10 = would hold a top-tier YouTube audience, 7 = acceptable, below 7 = must be fixed). Be honest and demanding; do not inflate scores.
Criteria (use exactly these ids):
- hook: the first 15 seconds create a burning question; no greeting, no slow start.
- open_loops: questions are opened and later closed; the viewer always has a reason to keep watching.
- rehooks: no long stretch without a reason to stay (in a long video a re-hook roughly every 60-90 seconds; in a Short, every sentence raises interest by itself).
- pacing: no sagging, no repetition, every paragraph moves the story forward.
- clarity: easy to follow by ear without prior knowledge; every character, place and reason is explained; the story can be retold in one sentence; no riddles or unexplained jumps.
- emotion: real stakes, empathy, tension, awe.
- payoff: the ending answers the hook's question and lands with impact.
- surprise: the story has a turn the viewer did not see coming, and the ending cannot be guessed from the first lines. For a Short this is critical: a predictable or advert-like story ("problem, the hero fixes it the obvious way, happy end") scores at most 4 and must be rewritten with an escalation, a real twist and a punchline.
- spoken: sounds like a person telling a story: complete, connected sentences of natural length; no telegraphic fragments, noun lists, verbless teaser lines or written-style constructions; nothing a voice actor would trip on.
- accuracy: no invented facts presented as real; uncertainty is acknowledged.
- length: matches the target length.

For every criterion write comment_ru in Russian (what is good, what is wrong, concretely).
In issues_ru list the concrete problems you will fix (in Russian).
In verdict_ru give a 2-3 sentence overall verdict in Russian.
